Holy Eucharist · Qiddase

Divine Liturgy

The Divine Liturgy (Qiddase) is the heart of the life of our Eparchy. The Catholic Eparchy of Keren celebrates it in the Ge’ez rite of the Alexandrian tradition, in Ge’ez and in the languages of our people.

Sundays & Feast Days

The Eucharist is celebrated in every parish on Sundays and on the great feasts of the Lord and of Our Lady.

The Anaphoras

The Ge’ez tradition is rich in Eucharistic prayers (anaphoras), such as the Anaphora of the Apostles and the Anaphora of Our Lady Mary.

Mass Times

Celebration times differ from parish to parish. Please contact your parish or see Parishes & Chapels.

Prayer of the Hours

Divine Office

The Divine Office is the prayer of the Church that sanctifies the hours of the day. In the Ge’ez tradition, the Hours and the sacred chant of St Yared unite priests, religious and faithful in the praise of God.

Hours of Prayer

Morning, midday and evening prayer, following the Ge’ez Book of Hours.

Sacred Chant (Zema)

The liturgical chant handed down from St Yared, sung by the deacons and choirs of our parishes.

Pray With Us

Monasteries, religious communities and parishes welcome all who wish to join the prayer of the Church.

Learning to Celebrate

Liturgical Formation

Liturgical formation helps the whole People of God to celebrate with understanding, devotion and active participation.

Deacons & Altar Servers

Training in the service of the altar, the Ge’ez readings and the order of the Qiddase.

Choirs & Chant

Schools of Ge’ez and Zema for young people and choir members.

Catechesis for the Faithful

Courses and talks on the meaning of the liturgy, the sacraments and the liturgical year.
