    Priests

    A priest’s day

    In the liturgy, the sacraments and the life of the community — from dawn to night.

    1. The day begins with the prayer of the Church in Ge’ez — the Hours that sanctify the morning.

    2. He celebrates the Qiddase with the people — the heart of every parish.

    3. Confession, baptisms, marriages and blessings for the families of the parish.

    4. He brings Communion and comfort to the sick, the elderly and families in hardship.

    5. Catechism for children, Bible study with young people and preparation for the sacraments.

    6. Meetings with the parish council, the catechists and the small Christian communities.

    7. The day closes as it began: in prayer for the people entrusted to him.

    A typical day — it changes with the parish and the season.

    Always learning

    A priest’s formation does not end at ordination.

    Days of recollection

    A day of silence, prayer and confession, often with the priests of the deanery.

    The annual retreat

    Several days apart with the bishop and fellow priests, to renew their calling.

    Studies and courses

    Theological studies and courses — in Eritrea and abroad — in Scripture, liturgy, canon law and pastoral care.

    Learning from one another

    Deanery meetings where priests share experience, plan together and support one another.

    Who stands with our priests

    Like an arch on its pillars, a priest’s ministry rests on the care of the whole Eparchy.
