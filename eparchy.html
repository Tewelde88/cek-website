    Belonging to the Eparchy

    From the first day of life to the last, the Eparchy walks with you.

    1. Welcomed at the font

      Baptism welcomes the child into God’s family, with the blessing of the whole parish.

      Read more

    2. Learning the faith

      Weekly catechism in every parish and preparation for First Communion.

      Read more

    3. Growing together

      Youth groups, retreats and Bible study, choirs and schools of Ge’ez and chant.

      Read more

    4. Answering God’s call

      Preparation for marriage, or discerning a call to the priesthood or religious life.

      Read more

    5. A family that prays

      The Sunday Liturgy, confession, the feasts and small Christian communities.

      Read more

    6. Never alone

      Anointing of the sick, visits to the elderly, and help for families in hardship through CESK.

      Read more

    7. Remembered in prayer

      The Church remembers the departed in prayer and in the Liturgy.
