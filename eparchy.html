Our Shepherd

Most Rev. Kidane Yebio

Bishop of Keren

Most Rev. Kidane Yebio, Bishop of Keren, has devoted his life to study, priestly ministry, pastoral service, and leadership. Born in Shnara, Eritrea, his journey from local schools and seminaries to advanced studies in Rome prepared him for decades of service to the Church and the communities of the Eparchy of Keren.
