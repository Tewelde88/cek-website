Charity in Action

About

CESK is the Caritas and social service office of the Catholic Eparchy of Keren. Inspired by the Gospel and the social teaching of the Church, it carries out the Eparchy’s works of charity and development together with parishes, religious communities and partners.

Human Dignity

Every person is created in the image of God and deserves respect, care and the chance to live a full life.

Preference for the Poor

We give first attention to those in greatest need — families in hardship, the sick, the elderly and children.

Solidarity

We work hand in hand with communities, so that help today becomes self-reliance tomorrow.
