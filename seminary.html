Forming Future Priests

The Path to the Priesthood

Tap each step to learn more.

Discernment

Prayer and conversation with your parish priest help you to recognise God’s call.

Minor Seminary

Young men discerning a call to the priesthood begin in the minor seminary, combining school studies with prayer and community life.

Major Seminary

Seminarians continue with philosophy and theology, preparing for ordination to the diaconate and the priesthood.

Diaconate

Ordained deacons, they serve at the liturgy, proclaim the Gospel and help the poor.

Priesthood

Ordained priests, they celebrate the sacraments and shepherd the People of God.
