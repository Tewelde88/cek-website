Liturgical Calendar

Feasts and fasts of the Catholic Eparchy of Keren according to the Ethiopian (Ge’ez) liturgical calendar, with the matching Gregorian dates.

Why a liturgical calendar?

A year lived with Christ

Through the year the Church walks with Christ — from his birth to his Passion and Resurrection — together with Mary and the saints. Our calendar follows the Ge’ez reckoning: it tells the faithful when to feast, when to fast and how to pray, so that every season becomes a way to God.

  • PrepareThe fasts — of the Prophets, of Nineveh, Great Lent, of the Apostles and of Filseta — times of prayer and conversion.
  • RememberThe angels and saints, month after month — Saint Michael on the 12th, Saint Gabriel on the 19th.
  • CelebrateThe great feasts of the Lord and of Mary — Meskel, Lidet, Timket, Fasika.
